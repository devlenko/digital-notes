# digital-notes
A web application that replicates the feeling of sticky notes.

## Features

### Adding and Deleting Notes
* **Creating a Note:** Press the **+** icon at the bottom right corner of the screen to add a new note.
* **Deleting All Notes:** Press the trash can icon at the bottom right corner of the screen to delete all of your notes. Be careful using this button, as your notes can not be retrieved after they have been deleted.

### Interacting with Notes
* **Moving a Note:** Click and drag the top of a note to move it around the screen.
* **Resizing a Note:** Click and drag the bottom right corner of a note to resize it. This lets you have more text on a note without needing to scroll.
* **Change Color:** Press the paintbrush icon at the top right corner of a note to change its color. You can cycle between yellow, red, blue, and green for each note.
* **Deleting a Single Note:** Press the **X** icon at the top right corner of a note to delete it. Be careful when deleting notes, as you can not recover them.

### Your Notes Are Saved
* Your notes, along with their positions, sizes, and text content, are all saved to your browser. That way, you can keep your notes just the way you like them.
